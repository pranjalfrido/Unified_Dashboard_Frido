Logistics & Ops, mobile: proposed

  • One title instead of the duplicated "LOGISTICS & OPS PERFORMANCE" heading + card title.
  • Clear date badge ("Showing 10 Sep – 10 Oct · latest complete data") instead of the long technical sentence.
  • Hero: total shipments plus a Delivered % ring.
  • Grouped tiles (Delivery health / Courier quality) with a colour edge: green = on target, amber = watch, red = act.
  • Plain names on mobile ("First-attempt success" instead of "FASR %"). Tap any tile for the definition.
  • ▲/▼ on every metric, consistent with the rule for the KPIs.
  • Speed & SLA in one card with Forward / RTO / Reverse tabs. Each shows the end-to-end days, stage averages as bars, and SLA met as progress bars with "x of y" counts. All 8 TAT metrics and all SLA thresholds are kept, just grouped by flow instead of one long grid.
  • Stage averages are not shown as a chain that adds up: they come from different shipment sets (0.8d + 3.3d ≠ 4.8d), so a chain would mislead.

State of the business: proposed

  • Customers: New vs Repeat revenue as one split bar (61% / 39%), so you see the mix instantly. Customers, Repeat rate and LTV sit in one even row, with no uneven card heights or empty gap.
  • Inventory alerts: Stockout risk and Dead stock as actionable rows. Tapping one opens Inventory Health already filtered.
  • Short "Updated 25 Oct, 2:33 pm" instead of "snapshot as of 25/10/2026, 14:33:13".
  • One font, one type scale, and every tile in a row has the same height.
