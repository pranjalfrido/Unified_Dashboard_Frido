Inventory, mobile: proposed

  • One header (shared design): "Inventory" with Inventory Health / Sales & Allocation sub-tabs. Inventory Health has no date picker — just "Updated … · sales till …" under the title (Regular facilities only on mobile); Sales & Allocation shows the date pill. All slicers (Location, Stock status, Avg sale window, Facility type, Facility, Category, Channel…) go into the filter icon.

Health

  • Summary card (no trend: it's a snapshot): total units + RTD / RAW / Blocked split bar; 6 tiles (RTD, RAW, Blocked RAW, Avg sale B2C, Total avg sale, DOI).
  • Warehouse health: 7 compact rows (status dot, units, RTD/RAW bar, DOI, allocation %) instead of a swipe carousel. Tap → detail sheet.
  • Stock explorer merges Inventory Detail + Location-wise table: drill-down Category → Sub-category → SKU with a breadcrumb, a location chip row (All, PNQ, GGN…) replacing the 7-location matrix, and a status filter in the "⋯" menu (Critical / Low / Sufficient / Excess). Each row: units, avg sale, DOI and a status-mix bar. Tap a SKU → sheet with RTD / RAW / blocked / DOI / status / website status + location split.
  • Ageing stock: Slow moving / Dead stock toggle, no truncated names. Spare Parts excluded (mobile and desktop).

Sales & Allocation

  • Master card with the trend + 6 tiles (Revenue, Units, ASP, Fill rate, Momentum, B2C mix); tap a tile to switch the chart.
  • Sales vs allocation: two thin bars per location (demand vs shipped).
  • Contribution: Channel / Category toggle + Units / Revenue chips (one card instead of two).
  • Top products and Biggest movers (Risers / Fallers, last vs 2nd-last / 7th-last) as ranked lists.
  • Daily sales by product: the date-column matrix becomes a drill-down list with a 7-day sparkline and total; tap → daily values.
