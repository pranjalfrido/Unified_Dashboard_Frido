Overview, mobile: proposed

  • One hero number with % change vs the previous period. "Prev Period" is no longer a separate KPI.
  • A clean chart: no X/Y axis labels, full width. The dashed line is the previous period.
  • Touch and drag on the chart: the big number shows that day's value and date. Let go to go back to the total.
  • Swipeable KPI chips: tap one to switch the chart and the hero number to that metric.
  • No Daily/Weekly/Monthly toggle. It's picked automatically from the date range; you can override it in "⋯".
  • Return %: going up shows red (up is bad).
  • Bottom nav: 5 labelled items + "More" (planned in Phase 2).
Mockup only. The chart shape and the % changes on the chips are illustrative. Real values come from your dashboard. The headline totals are taken from your screenshot.
