Performance

  • Master card: Total shipments + GMV / Delivered / RTO line; chart; 10 tiles (RTO %, Z-RTO, FASR, RASR, multi-attempt, processing, pickup, in-transit, fulfilment, RTO TAT) with desktop's ▲/▼. Tap a tile → chart.
  • Courier scorecard: View by Courier / Zone / Facility / Month — tap a 2nd one to nest (e.g. Courier → Zone: each courier expands into zones). Show chips Tier I / II / III / COD / Prepaid are multi-select filters (e.g. Tier I + II combined). Sort chips for all columns. No duplicate total box (it's in the KPIs).
  • Speed: the 4 TAT bucket tables become one card with a toggle and a stacked bucket bar per facility/courier.
  • Weight, Geography (drop city / pickup city / drop state), RTO (ageing / delivery ageing / reasons as a ranked list, no rotated labels) and NDR (All / COD / Prepaid) as compact lists with detail sheets.

Cost

  • Overview: total cost with a B2C / FTL / 3PL split bar, 6 tiles, monthly trend, and one partners card with a B2C / FTL / 3PL toggle.

  • FTL/PTL: ₹60.48 L + 8 tiles, transporters, vehicle types, lanes (search), rate consistency.
  • 3PL: ₹57.77 L with an Operations / Rental split bar + 8 tiles (₹/parcel, ₹/kg, parcel weight, ₹/site/month, operations, rental, % of value); partners with spend vs parcel share double bars (replaces the Spend vs volume chart); warehouse sites (search); site trend with one site at a time (chips) against the blended rate (replaces the 6-line chart).
